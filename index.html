Privacy Policy for Missionary Resource Center

Last Updated: December 7, 2024

Introduction

Missionary Resource Center ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

1. Personal Information

  • Email Address: Used for account authentication and identification
  • Account Credentials: Password (encrypted and stored securely via Firebase Authentication)
  • Profile Information: Name and role (missionary or admin) stored in our database

2. Location Information

We collect precise location data to provide the following features:

  • Prayer Time Calculations: Calculate accurate Islamic prayer times based on your geographic coordinates
  • Prayer Notifications: Send timely prayer reminders based on your current location
  • Service Area Tracking: Track missionary service areas for organizational purposes
Location Data Collection:
  • When the app is in use (foreground)
  • When the app is in the background (only for prayer time notifications)
  • Location data is processed locally on your device
  • Location coordinates are not shared with third parties
  • You can disable location permissions at any time in your device settings

3. Usage Data

  • App Activity: Features you use and how you interact with the app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • Error Logs: Crash reports and error logs for app improvement

4. Content You Create

  • Contacts: Missionary contact information you add or view
  • Circulars: Documents and announcements you access
  • Amendments: Updates and changes you review

How We Use Your Information

Primary Uses:

  1. Authentication: Verify your identity and manage your account
  2. Access Control: Ensure only authorized missionaries can access the app
  3. Prayer Times: Calculate and notify you of Islamic prayer times
  4. Resource Sharing: Provide access to missionary resources, contacts, and circulars
  5. App Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve functionality
  6. Security: Detect and prevent unauthorized access

We Do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data for advertising purposes
  • Track your location for marketing
  • Access your data for purposes other than app functionality

Data Storage and Security

Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase for:

  • Authentication: Secure user login and password management
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted storage of user data and app content
  • Security: Industry-standard encryption and security protocols

Security Measures:

  • All data transmitted is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Firestore security rules prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access limited to whitelisted email addresses only

Data Retention:

  • Active accounts: Data retained as long as account is active
  • Inactive accounts: Data retained for organizational records
  • Deletion requests: Contact us to request account deletion

Third-Party Services

Firebase (Google LLC)

  • Purpose: Authentication, database, and security
  • Data Shared: Email, encrypted password, user profile
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We Do Not Use:

  • Analytics tracking services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Third-party marketing tools

User Rights and Controls

You Have the Right To:

  1. Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  2. Correction: Update or correct your information
  3. Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  4. Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  5. Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  6. Objection: Object to certain data processing activities

How to Exercise Your Rights:

Contact us at: faheem.arshad@ahmadiyya.us

Location Control:

You can control location permissions through:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Missionary Resource Center
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Missionary Resource Center → Permissions → Location

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by adult missionaries of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

Access Control and Whitelist

  • Only authorized email addresses can create accounts
  • Whitelist managed by organization administrators
  • Access can be revoked at any time by administrators
  • Users are notified if their access is deactivated

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Share Data Only When:

  1. Required by Law: Legal obligations, court orders, or government requests
  2. Organization Administration: With authorized administrators for user management
  3. Service Providers: With Firebase/Google for app infrastructure (bound by strict privacy agreements)
  4. Safety: To protect the rights, property, or safety of users or others

We Never:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Share data for marketing purposes
  • Disclose data to unauthorized parties
  • Use data for purposes outside this policy

International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States where Firebase servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in compliance with applicable laws.
